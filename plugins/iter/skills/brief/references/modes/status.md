# Status Mode

## Input

Historical `status_window`, current open state, relevant task or worktree
progress, and the requested audience or cadence.

## Workflow

1. Relate meaningful changes and unresolved commitments to current goals: what
   advanced, what became possible or blocked, and what needs reconsideration.
2. Reconcile apparent failures with newer evidence for the same work.
3. Identify decisions and reviews actually due, including quiet work, while
   keeping ongoing autonomous investigation visible.

## Output

Explain where the work stands and why it matters now, with supporting evidence,
risks, and decisions. Show where human attention would change the outcome. Scale
detail to significance; if healthy with no action due, say so briefly.

## Rules

- A newer successful attempt supersedes a failure only when workflow, branch,
  and commit match.
