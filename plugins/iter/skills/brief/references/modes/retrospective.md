# Retrospective Mode

## Input

Historical `retrospective_window`, stated direction, approved intentions,
observed results, and earlier improvement decisions.

## Workflow

1. Establish the approved baseline from recorded decisions or linked artifacts.
2. Compare outcomes with intentions and direction. Assess both execution and
   whether the chosen work was worthwhile.
3. Explain material deviations, rework, and decision/review delays from evidence.
4. Revisit previous improvements: implementation status, observed effect, and
   approved work still unfinished. Derive what to continue, change, or stop.

## Output

Assess progress toward intended outcomes, explain material differences, and give
supported recommendations. Separate outstanding approved actions from new
proposals; use only sections warranted by the evidence.

## Rules

- Without approved intentions, state **Missing baseline** and assess observed
  results without inventing commitments. Linked decisions may preserve the
  baseline after temporary artifacts are removed.
- Label uncertain causes; recurring friction requires repeated evidence.
