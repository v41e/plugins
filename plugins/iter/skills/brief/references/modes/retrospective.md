# Retrospective Mode

## Purpose

Compare outcomes with approved intent and identify what to continue, change, or stop.

## Input

Historical `retrospective_window`, direction, approved intentions, observed
results, and earlier improvement decisions.

## Workflow

1. Establish the approved baseline from recorded decisions, prior briefs and
   human responses, or linked artifacts. Preserve dated amendments rather than
   treating a later decision as the original commitment.
2. Compare outcomes with intentions and direction. Assess execution and whether
   the chosen work was worthwhile.
3. Explain material deviations, rework, and decision or review delays from
   evidence. Revisit previous improvements and approved work still unfinished.

## Output

Assess progress toward intended outcomes, explain material differences, and
recommend what to continue, change, or stop. Separate outstanding approved
work from new proposals; use only sections warranted by the evidence.

## Rules

- Without approved intentions, state **Missing baseline** and assess observed
  results without inventing commitments. Linked decisions can preserve the
  baseline after temporary artifacts are removed.
- Label uncertain causes; recurring friction requires repeated evidence.
